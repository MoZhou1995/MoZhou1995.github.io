Course Description

Calculus C is a foundational course for students in the life sciences, medicine, agriculture, and selected science, engineering, economics, management, and social science programs that require university-level mathematics. The course develops a working understanding of the central concepts, theory, and computational methods of calculus, together with abstract reasoning, logical argument, spatial intuition, and mathematical problem-solving skills.

Main Topics

  • Precalculus foundations: functions, limits, and continuity
  • Derivatives and differentials
  • Mean value theorems and applications of differentiation
  • Indefinite integrals and basic integration techniques
  • Definite integrals, the fundamental theorem of calculus, and applications
  • Analytic geometry in space and vector algebra

Textbook and References

Textbook: Zhou Jianying and Zhang Jinyan, 《高等数学（上）》 (Higher Mathematics, Vol. 1), Peking University Press.

Problem-solving reference: Zhou Jianying and Li Zhengyuan, 《高等数学解题指南》, Peking University Press.
