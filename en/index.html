Mo Zhou

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Information and Computational Sciences, School of Mathematical Sciences, Peking University, and a member of the Committee on Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence of the Chinese Mathematical Society. I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from Duke University in 2023, advised by Professor Jianfeng Lu, and my B.S. in Mathematics from Tsinghua University in 2018. From 2023 to 2026, I conducted research and taught at UCLA under the mentorship of Professor Stanley Osher.

My research lies at the intersection of machine learning, control theory, and scientific computing, with a focus on stochastic optimal control, mean field control and games, reinforcement learning, normalizing flows, and generative models.
