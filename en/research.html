Research

My research lies at the intersection of machine learning and control theory. I develop efficient algorithms and rigorous analysis for stochastic optimal control, mean field control, and mean field games. I am also interested in reinforcement learning and scientific machine learning for high-dimensional partial differential equations, including neural ordinary differential equations, normalizing flows, and generative modeling.

  • Stochastic optimal control
  • Mean field control and games
  • Reinforcement learning
  • Deep learning for PDEs
  • Generative modeling and normalizing flows
